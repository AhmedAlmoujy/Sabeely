SUBJECT AREAS

What You Will Learn

Seeking sacred knowledge is a continuous lifelong journey. It begins with clear learning, matures through understanding, and transforms into practice, noble character, and spiritual peace.

Aqeedah (Islamic Creed)

Explore the core pillars of Islamic belief and develop a sound, confident understanding of faith rooted in authentic Quran and Sunnah.

Fiqh (Jurisprudence)

Learn practical Islamic rulings regarding daily worship (Taharah, Salah, Sawm, Zakah) and essential everyday interactions.

Hadith Studies

Study key sayings and traditions of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, extracting timeless guidance, legal insights, and moral wisdom.

Seerah (Prophetic Biography)

Follow the inspiring life journey of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, understanding key historical milestones and moral lessons for modern living.

Islamic Character & Adab

Learn Islamic etiquette, personal manners, and social ethics designed to elevate your character and treatment of others.

Foundations of Islam

A balanced starting overview for new Muslims or beginners seeking a solid, holistic understanding of the Islamic worldview.

Islamic Studies FAQs

Are these Islamic Studies classes suitable for beginners?

Yes. Beginners start with the foundational principles of Islam and gradually progress toward deeper subjects like Fiqh, Seerah, and Hadith.

Can I study Islamic Studies alongside Quran or Arabic?

Yes! Many students combine Islamic Studies with Quran recitation or Arabic language learning to build a well-rounded Islamic education.

Are there specialized tracks for children or reverts?

Yes. We have customized age-appropriate learning tracks for youth as well as supportive foundational modules for new Muslims (reverts).
