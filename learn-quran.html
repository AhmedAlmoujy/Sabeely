FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quran Program FAQs

Can beginners with zero Arabic background learn Quran?

Yes. Beginners start with basic Quranic Arabic phonetics (Norani Qaida / Baghdadi method) and gradually progress to reading actual verses.

Can I focus specifically on Tajweed rules?

Absolutely. If you already read basic Quran, we tailor your classes to focus on practical and theoretical Tajweed rules to refine your recitation.

How does Quran memorization (Hifz) work online?

You meet 1-on-1 with your tutor to recite new memorization lines, recite recent lesson pages (Sabaqi), and review older parts (Manzil) to guarantee total retention.

Are there female tutors available for sisters and children?

Yes! We have qualified male and female certified Quran tutors available for all age groups and preferences.
