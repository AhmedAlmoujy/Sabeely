FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Learning Arabic FAQs

Can I learn Arabic from absolute scratch?

Yes, absolutely! You can begin your journey from zero prior knowledge. We start with letter shapes, phonetics, and basic pronunciation in a supportive step-by-step manner.

Are these lessons suitable for non-native Arabic speakers?

Yes. Our Arabic curriculum is specifically designed for non-native speakers, utilizing clear English explanations, visual aids, and gradual practice.

Will learning Arabic help me understand the Quran?

Yes! Learning Arabic provides the grammatical and vocabulary keys to understand Quranic words, structures, and meanings directly without relying solely on translation.

What if I already know some basic Arabic?

If you already have some background knowledge, we place you at an intermediate level so you can focus directly on conversation, advanced grammar, or Quranic comprehension.
